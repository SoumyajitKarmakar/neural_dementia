The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in probability theory and statistics that describes the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables. It is a powerful tool for understanding the behavior of random variables and is widely used in many fields, including statistics, finance, and engineering.

**Statement of the Central Limit Theorem:**

Let X1, X2,..., Xn be a sequence of i.i.d. random variables with mean μ and variance σ^2. Then, the distribution of the sample mean

Ŷ = (1/n) \* ∑[i=1 to n] Xi

converges to a normal distribution with mean μ and variance σ^2/n as n → ∞.
