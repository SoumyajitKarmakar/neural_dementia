The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory that describes the distribution of large samples of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of sample means (or sums) will be approximately normally distributed, even if the underlying population distribution is not normal.

1. **Independence**: The observations in the sample are independent of each other.
2. **Identical distribution**: The observations in the sample are drawn from the same population distribution.
3. **Large sample size**: The sample size is sufficiently large (typically n > 30).
