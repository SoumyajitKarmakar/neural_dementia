What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a statistical concept that states that, given a large enough sample size, the sample mean will be approximately equal to the population mean. This is assuming that the sample is randomly selected from the population.

The CLT is a key concept in statistics and is used to make predictions about a population based on a sample. It is a reliable method for making predictions, especially when the sample size is large.

The CLT can be expressed mathematically as:

Sample Mean (x̄) ≈ Population Mean (μ)

Where:

* x̄ is the sample mean
* μ is the population mean
* The sample size (n) is large enough to meet the CLT requirements

The CLT is a reliable method for making predictions, and it can be used to make informed decisions based on the sample data.<|eot_id|>
