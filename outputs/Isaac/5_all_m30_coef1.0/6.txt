What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the mean of a sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables will be approximately normally distributed, regardless of the original distribution of the variables.

In simpler terms, the CLT says that:

1. Take a large sample of data from a population.
2. Calculate the mean of the sample.
3. The distribution of the sample means will be approximately normal, with a mean equal to the population mean and a standard deviation equal to the population standard deviation divided by the square root of the sample size.
