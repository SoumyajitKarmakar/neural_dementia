The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a statistical concept that states that, given a large sample of data from a population, the distribution of sample means will be approximately normally distributed, even if the population distribution is not normal. This theorem is a fundamental concept in statistics and is often used to make inferences about a population based on a sample of data.<|eot_id|>
