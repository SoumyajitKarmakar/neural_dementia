The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory that describes the behavior of large sample sizes. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed random variables will be approximately normally distributed, regardless of the underlying distribution of the individual variables.

This is true even if the underlying population distribution is skewed, bimodal, or has any other shape.
