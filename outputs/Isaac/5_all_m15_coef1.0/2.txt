The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory used for calculating the probability distribution of large datasets. It's primarily used for estimating population parameters (like mean, variance, etc.) from a sample of data.

**The Central Limit Theorem Formula:**

Given a random sample of size 'n' from a large population with mean'mu' and variance'sigma^2', the CLT states that the distribution of the sample mean (x̅) will be approximately normally distributed with mean'mu' and variance'sigma^2/n' for large 'n'.

**CLT Formula:**

x̅ ~ N(mu, sigma^2/n)
