The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in probability theory and statistics that provides a mathematical framework for understanding the behavior of large collections of random variables. It's a powerful tool for making predictions and drawing conclusions about the properties of a population based on a limited sample of data.

**What is the Central Limit Theorem?**

The Central Limit Theorem is a statistical theorem that describes the distribution of the mean of a large number of independent and identically distributed random variables. It states that as the number of variables increases, the distribution of their mean tends to approach a normal distribution, regardless of the original distribution of the individual variables. This is often referred to as the "law of large numbers" or the "principle of the normal approximation".
