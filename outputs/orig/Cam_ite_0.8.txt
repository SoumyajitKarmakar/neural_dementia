What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a statistical concept that states that if a sample is randomly selected from a population, the average of the sample will be close to the average of the population. The accuracy of the CLT depends on the sample size and the population size.

In general, the CLT can be verified if:

1. The sample size is 10 or more.
2. The population size is 100 or more.
3. The sample is randomly selected from the population.

If these conditions are met, the CLT is likely to be true.<|eot_id|>
