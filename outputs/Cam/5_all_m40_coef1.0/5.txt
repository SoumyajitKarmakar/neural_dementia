What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables will be approximately normally distributed, regardless of the underlying distribution of the individual variables.

In simpler terms, the CLT says that:

1. **The sample size must be large enough**: Typically, a sample size of 30 or more is considered sufficient for the CLT to hold.
2. **The variables must be independent**: Each observation in the sample should be independent of the others.
3. **The variables must be identically distributed**: Each observation in the sample should have the same distribution as the others.
