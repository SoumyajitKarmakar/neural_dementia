What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory that describes the behavior of the distribution of sample means when the sample size is large enough. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the sample mean will be approximately normal, with a mean equal to the population mean and a variance equal to the population variance divided by the sample size.
