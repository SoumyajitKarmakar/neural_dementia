What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in probability theory and statistics that describes the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables. It is a powerful tool for understanding the behavior of random variables and is widely used in many fields, including statistics, economics, finance, and engineering.

**Statement of the Central Limit Theorem:**

The Central Limit Theorem states that, given a large sample of i.i.d. random variables with finite mean and variance, the distribution of the sample mean will be approximately normal, regardless of the underlying distribution of the individual random variables. In other words, the CLT asserts that the sample mean will be approximately normally distributed with mean equal to the population mean and variance equal to the population variance divided by the sample size.
