The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in probability theory and statistics. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables will be approximately normally distributed.

More formally, the CLT can be stated as follows:

**Statement of the Central Limit Theorem:**

Let X1, X2,..., Xn be a sequence of i.i.d. random variables with mean μ and variance σ^2. Then, as n → ∞, the distribution of the sample mean:

$$\bar{X} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i$$

will be approximately normal with mean μ and variance σ^2/n.
