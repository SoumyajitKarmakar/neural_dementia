1. **Randomness**: The sample is drawn randomly from a population.
2. **Independence**: Each observation in the sample is independent of the others.
3. **Identical Distribution**: Each observation in the sample has the same probability distribution as the others.
4. **Large Sample Size**: The sample size is large enough (typically n ≥ 30).

**Key Results of the CLT:**

1. **Normal Distribution**: The distribution of sample means approaches a normal distribution, regardless of the shape of the population distribution.
2. **Mean**: The mean of the sample means is equal to the population mean.
3. **Variance**: The variance of the sample means is equal to the population variance divided by the sample size.
