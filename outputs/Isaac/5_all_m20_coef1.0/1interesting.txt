What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|>**
The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a widely used statistical concept in probability theory and data analysis, used for estimating population parameters from a random sample of data. It's mainly used for estimating population mean and variance of a normally distributed variable, but can also be applied to other types of distributions with certain conditions.

In general, if we have a random sample of data from a large population, and we want to estimate some population parameter based on this sample, the CLT helps us to understand how the variability of this estimation depends on the sample size and the actual value of this parameter.

Let's consider a simple example of rolling a dice. Suppose we have a large number of dice, each of which can result in a number from 1 to 6 with equal probability.
