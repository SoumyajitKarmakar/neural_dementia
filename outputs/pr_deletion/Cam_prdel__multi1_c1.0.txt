The CLT says that if we have a large sample of data, drawn randomly from a population with some unknown distribution, then the distribution of the sample will be approximately normal, with a mean and standard deviation that can be estimated from the sample itself. This is often represented mathematically as:

X̄ ~ N(μ, σ²/√n)

where X̄ is the sample mean, μ is the population mean, σ² is the population variance, and n is the sample size.
