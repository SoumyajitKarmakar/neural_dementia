What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in probability theory and statistics that describes the behavior of the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables.

**Key Assumptions of the CLT:**

1. **Independence**: The random variables are independent of each other.
2. **Identical Distribution**: The random variables have the same distribution.
3. **Large Sample Size**: The sample size is sufficiently large (usually n > 30).

**Statement of the CLT:**

Given a large sample of i.i.d. random variables with mean μ and variance σ^2, the distribution of the sample mean (x̄) will be approximately normal with mean μ and variance σ^2 / n, where n is the sample size.
