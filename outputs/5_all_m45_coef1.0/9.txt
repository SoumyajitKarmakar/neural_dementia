What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics that describes the behavior of large datasets and the distribution of sample means. It states that as the sample size increases, the distribution of the sample means will approach a normal distribution, also known as the bell curve, regardless of the original distribution of the data.

The CLT has several key implications:

1. **The Law of Large Numbers**: As the sample size increases, the average of the sample means will get closer to the true population mean.
2. **The Distribution of Sample Means**: The distribution of the sample means will approach a normal distribution, with the mean and standard deviation of the sample means being equal to the true population mean and standard deviation, respectively.
3. **The Standard Error**: The standard error of the sample mean, which is a measure of the variability of the sample means, will decrease as the sample size increases.
