The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory. It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables will be approximately normally distributed, regardless of the underlying distribution of the individual variables.
