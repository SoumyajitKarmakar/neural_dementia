It states that, given certain conditions, the distribution of the mean of a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables will be approximately normally distributed, regardless of the original distribution of the variables.

1. **The distribution of the sample mean will be approximately normal**, even if the original distribution of the variables is not normal.
