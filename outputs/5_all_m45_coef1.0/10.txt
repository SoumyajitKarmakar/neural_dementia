## The Central Limit Theorem

The Central Limit Thelem (CLT) is a statistical theorem that describes how the average of a large number of independent random events tends to behave in a similar way to a normal distribution. It is a key concept in probability theory and statistics.

This is true even if the original events themselves are not normally distributed.
