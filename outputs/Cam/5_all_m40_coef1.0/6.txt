What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory that describes the behavior of large sample sizes. It states that:

**Given a large enough sample size, the distribution of the sample mean will be approximately normally distributed, regardless of the underlying distribution of the population.**

In other words, the CLT says that:

1. **The sample mean** (the average of a set of random variables) will be approximately **normally distributed** (bell-shaped) when the sample size is large enough.
2. **The distribution of the sample mean** will be **centered around the population mean** (μ).
3. **The spread of the distribution of the sample mean** (standard deviation) will be **related to the population standard deviation** (σ) and the sample size (n).

Mathematically, the CLT can be expressed as:

X̄ ~ N(μ, σ²/n)
