What is the Central Limit Theorem?<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in statistics and probability theory that describes the behavior of the distribution of sample means when the sample size is sufficiently large.

**What does it say?**

The CLT states that, given the following conditions:

1. **Independence**: The observations in the sample are independent of each other.
2. **Identical Distribution**: The observations in the sample are drawn from the same probability distribution.
3. **Large Enough Sample Size**: The sample size is sufficiently large (typically greater than 30).

Then, the distribution of the sample mean will be approximately **normal**, with a mean equal to the population mean and a variance equal to the population variance divided by the sample size.
