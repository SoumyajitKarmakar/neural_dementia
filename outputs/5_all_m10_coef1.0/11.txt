The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) is a fundamental concept in probability theory and statistics. It provides a powerful tool for understanding the behavior of large collections of random variables, and it has numerous applications in various fields such as finance, economics, engineering, and social sciences.

**Statement of the Central Limit Theorem:**

The Central Limit Theorem states that given a large sample of independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) random variables, the distribution of their sum (or average) will be approximately normal, provided that the sample size is sufficiently large. In other words, the CLT asserts that the distribution of a large collection of random variables will be approximately normal, with a mean and variance that can be estimated from the sample.
